The plot shows that there is a slight change in the slope suggesting that perhaps there was an impact from streaming services on the growth of box office revenue, there is also a change in the intercept of the cutpoint, further highlighting this point. As we have chosen to use all the data including during Covid, this may be another reason for the change in slope. In the next section we will calculate the appropriate bandwidth and calculate the change more locally. This will likely remove the effect of the lower revenues of covid.

Conclusion and Next Steps

The analysis shows that based on the available data there was no significant change in the box office revenues pre and post streaming services taking off. This is interesting as many people within the industry are saying that there has been an effect.

While we cannot argue with the outcome of this analysis it is important to recognize any potential floors in this analysis, or other variables which may be affecting the outcome. We have spoken about covid as having a large effect on box office revenue in 2020 and perhaps beyond, however there may have been other events which we have not considered which have had similar effects (either positively or negatively) which we are not aware of or have not considered. Knowing whether this data was completely unaffected by outside variables is both impossible and highly unlikely.

While thinking about this we can consider the assumptions of RDA: 1. The treatment variable impacts the outcome, but not any of the other variables. 2. The treatment assignment happens only at ONE cutpoint value of the forcing variable. 3. Treatment assignment is independent of the potential outcomes within a narrow interval around the cutpoint. 4. Counterfactual outcomes can be modeled within the interval around the cutpoint.

With such a large dataset it hard to be sure we meet these variables. Furthermore with regard to the second assumption, as the use and popularity of streaming services has been increasing since 2011 this may indicate that there is not a clear cutoff point and perhaps fuzzy RDA would have been a better choice for this analysis.

A final point to consider is are there alternative causal analysis methods that could be used which would be more appropriate. This project has been performed as part of my education in data science and therefore my knowledge of other methods may increase in the future to understand how this could be approched better.
